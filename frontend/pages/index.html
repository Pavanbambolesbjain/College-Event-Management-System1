FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event?
Create an account, browse events, and click "Register" on any open event. You'll instantly get a QR code pass for check-in.
Is CampusConnect free for students?
Yes! CampusConnect is completely free for students. Register with your college email and start discovering events immediately.
What is the QR Check-In system?
After registration, you receive a unique QR code ticket. Organizers scan it at the event entrance to instantly verify attendance — no paper required.
Can I cancel my registration?
Yes, you can cancel your registration from your dashboard before the event starts. The seat will be freed up for other students.
How do I get my participation certificate?
After attending an event and being marked present by the organizer, a digital certificate is automatically available in your dashboard under "My Certificates".
How can my club create and manage events?
Club organizers can log in through the Admin Portal to create events, manage registrations, run the QR scanner, and export attendance reports.
